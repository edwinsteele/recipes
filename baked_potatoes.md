# Baked Potatoes (Microwave + Oven)

# Instructions

0. Heat oven to 200C (fan forced)
1. Take 5 large potatoes (ideally Desiree) and puncture them with a fork 6-10 times each
2. Place them in the white microwaveable crockery baking dish
3. Cook them in the microwave (1000W) for 15 minutes, turning after 5 and 10 minutes, and moving outer to inner so everyone gets a turn on the outer.
4. Leave them for 3-5 minutes after microwaving (to let heat go to the centre)
5. Using a basting brush cover them with olive oil, and lightly salt
6. Put on a cake rack over an oven tray and place in the oven for 10 minutes
