1. **Loosen the noodles**: Knead the unopened 200 g pouch a few seconds so the strands separate, then slit the top.
2. **Blanch (30 s – 1 min)**: Bring a small saucepan of unsalted water to a rolling boil (about 1 L so they’re not crowded). Drop the noodles in, stir once, and simmer just long enough to heat through (< 1 min).
4. **Make the broth (takes 10 s)**: In a measuring jug mix 1 part kombu tsuyu : 7 parts fresh boiling water.  Example for one 200 g pack:• 40 ml tsuyu (≈ 2 Tbsp + 2 tsp)• 280 ml boiling water
5. **Make additions**: Cook for about a minute, then add 1 raw egg and cook for 45 seconds more.
3. Drain well**
Tip the noodles into a sieve, shake off excess water and put them straight into your serving bowl.
6. **Eat**
