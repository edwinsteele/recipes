# Slow Cooker Beef Bourguignon

Adapted from [The Recipe Critic](https://therecipecritic.com/wprm_print/slow-cooker-beef-bourguignon)

## Ingredients

- 200g diced bacon
- 1kg boneless beef chuck cut to 3cm inch cubes
- 250ml red cooking wine
- 500ml chicken broth
- 125ml Tomato Puree/Passata
- 60ml of low-salt soy sauce
- 1/4 cup flour
- 2 teaspoons dried thyme
- 2 small bay leaves
- 5 medium sliced carrots
- 500g baby potatoes or small desiree
- 200g sliced fresh mushrooms (button or swiss brown)
- fresh chopped parsley for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a large pan, cook diced bacon for 5-7 minutes or until crisp. Add the cooked bacon to the slow cooker.
2. Add beef pieces to the pan (no need to season, already enough salt in the bacon and pepper wasn't received well). Brown the beef on each side for 2-3 minutes. Transfer the beef to the slow cooker.
3. Add wine to the pan, scraping down the brown bits on the side. Allow it to simmer and reduce a bit (best to err on the side of more liquid than less i.e. don't reduce for too long)
4. Slowly add chicken broth, tomato passata, and soy sauce. Slowly whisk in flour. Pour the sauce into the slow cooker. Best to err on the side of more liquid than less.
5. Add thyme, bay leaves, carrots, potatoes, and fresh mushrooms to the slow cooker.
6. Stir well and cook on LOW for 8-10 hours or on HIGH for 6 hours. Cook until the beef is tender.

Top with fresh chopped parsley for garnish and serve with pasta or mashed potatoes. It's too rich to serve just with bread.
