# Time

15m prep time
1h 15m cooking time

# Ingredients

* 2 tablespoons Cobham onion & garlic infused 
* 1 leek, finely sliced
* 2 celery sticks, grated (or finely sliced)
* 2 small carrots, grated (or finely chopped)
* 2 x 400g Mutti peel tomatoes
* 200ml red wine
* 400g tin brown lentils, drained
* 700ml chicken stock
* 4 thick sausages or 6 thin sausages, sliced thinly

# Instructions

1. Heat large pot with 1 tablespoon of oil. Fry leek, celery and carrot until soft.
2. Add tomatoes and cook until mixture begins to stick to the bottom of the pan
3. Grill the sausages while the mixture is cooking. Once they are done, chop them up and put aside.
4. Add wine and cook until mixture is reduced by half. Add lentils and stock and cook for another 20 minutes.
5. Once step 4 is done, add sausage to the pot and cook altogether for 15 minutes.
